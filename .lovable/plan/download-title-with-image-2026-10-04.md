# Download title with image

## What will change
- Replace the current raw-image download with a download action that creates the final Instagram graphic in the browser.
- Draw the generated picture in a 4:5 canvas, add the same dark lower fade, and place the current title over it in large uppercase display text.
- Keep caption and hashtags outside the downloaded image.

## Validation
- Generate a post, download its image, and confirm the saved PNG includes the title and background without clipping.
- Check the app remains error-free.
